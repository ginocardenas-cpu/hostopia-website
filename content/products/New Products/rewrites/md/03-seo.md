## 2 · ACCORDION — "Your SMB customers can…"

### Follow a clear, AI-guided SEO plan
SEO intimidates most small business owners because the advice they find online is either too generic or too technical. Hostopia's AI-powered SEO Strategy analyzes each customer's website, industry, and local market, then generates a prioritized, step-by-step plan they can actually follow. Weekly tasks are bite-sized and specific — update this title tag, fix this broken link, add this keyword — so progress happens even when the owner only has 20 minutes a day. It's like having an SEO consultant in their pocket, built into the services they already buy from you.

### Discover the keywords that drive real traffic
Ranking for the wrong keywords is worse than not ranking at all. Hostopia's AI Keyword Discovery surfaces the terms your customers' actual buyers are searching for, with search volume, competition, and difficulty scores in plain language. Built-in tracking shows which keywords are gaining ground, which are slipping, and where the biggest opportunities sit. Whether your customer is a local plumber, a regional retailer, or a multi-location service business, they get a keyword strategy grounded in real search behavior — not guesswork.

### Publish SEO-friendly content without a copywriter
Fresh, optimized content is one of the strongest signals search engines use to rank a site — and one of the hardest things for small businesses to keep up with. Hostopia's AI Content Creation helps owners generate blog posts, service pages, and product descriptions that are search-ready from the first draft. Content is built around their target keywords, tuned to their brand voice, and ready to publish in minutes instead of days. Consistency becomes realistic, even for a one-person business.

### Optimize their website without touching code
Technical SEO fixes often stall because owners don't know what to change or how to change it. Hostopia's Website Optimization tool scans every page and turns technical issues — slow load times, missing meta tags, weak headings, thin content — into simple, plain-English recommendations. Each fix is prioritized by impact, so your customers work on what moves rankings first and skip what doesn't matter. For partners, that means fewer support escalations and more customers who stick around because they see results.

### See what's working and where to push harder
SEO is a long game, but progress should never feel invisible. Hostopia's Performance Tracking gives your customers a live view of rankings, organic traffic, top-performing pages, and how their site compares to local competitors. Easy-to-read dashboards show what's trending up, what's flat, and where the biggest wins are hiding — so they can double down on what works and cut what doesn't. Real data. Real progress. Real reasons to keep subscribing.
