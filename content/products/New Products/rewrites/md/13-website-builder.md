## 2 · ACCORDION — "With OPB, your SMB customers can…"

### Launch a professional site in minutes
Traditional website projects stall for weeks between "we need a site" and "we published a site." AI changes that math completely. With Online Presence Builder, a customer enters a business name, category, and a few details — and OPB generates a complete, multi-page website with industry-relevant copy, curated imagery, maps, and contact forms in under five minutes. The first draft is already good enough to publish, which means customers actually finish instead of abandoning halfway through. For partners, that's the difference between selling websites and activating them.

### Create real content without creative skills
Most SMBs don't have a copywriter, a photographer, or a designer — and hiring one costs more than the website. OPB's built-in AI writing and image generation fills that gap entirely. Owners type a prompt, AI produces homepage copy, about-page narrative, service descriptions, and matching imagery tailored to their industry. No more staring at blank pages, no more placeholder Latin, no more stock photos that don't fit. The output looks intentional, reads professionally, and represents the business the owner actually runs.

### Convert visitors into customers, not just impressions
A digital brochure doesn't grow a business. OPB sites come with the conversion tools SMBs need by default — lead capture forms, clear CTAs, Google Maps integration, galleries, testimonial blocks, and social widgets — so every visitor has a clear path to contact, book, or buy. And when the business is ready to sell online, a single click activates the integrated Online Store. Your customers get sites that generate business, and you get the attach path straight into ecommerce.

### Manage everything from one branded dashboard
SMBs hate logging into four different portals to manage their website, domain, email, and billing. Hostopia consolidates all of it into a single, partner-branded dashboard where customers edit their site, update DNS, check email, and manage subscriptions in one place. That consolidation is what makes your platform sticky — the more services live under one login, the more painful it becomes to switch, and the more natural every upsell conversation becomes.

### Scale from a first website to a full digital stack
OPB isn't the end of the journey — it's the beginning. The same platform that launched the site supports ecommerce, SEO, directory listings, reputation management, and marketing expansion as the business grows. Unlimited pages, per-page SEO controls, Google Analytics integration, and hosted infrastructure all ship standard, which means customers never outgrow the platform before they're ready to buy more from you.
