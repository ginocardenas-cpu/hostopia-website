## 2 · ACCORDION — "Your SMB customers get…"

### Data protection that works the moment the site goes live
Every login form, checkout page, and contact submission flows between a browser and a server — and without encryption, anyone on the network can read it. Hostopia's SSL certificates apply 128-bit encryption by default, protecting sensitive information in transit across every page of the site. Owners don't have to think about it, configure it, or monitor it. It simply works from the moment the certificate is installed, and it shields customers from the kind of data exposure that ends small businesses overnight.

### Visible trust signals that convert more visitors
Security is only valuable if visitors can see it. Hostopia's SSL certificates activate the browser padlock, enable clickable site seals, and (for EV certificates) trigger the green address bar that signals verified identity. Those signals do real work — converting hesitant visitors into customers, validating checkout pages, and telling return visitors the site is the real one. For SMBs competing with bigger brands, visible trust is the cheapest and fastest credibility upgrade available.

### PCI compliance support without a compliance team
Accepting credit cards online requires meeting PCI security standards, and SSL is the first requirement on the list. Hostopia's certificates support the baseline encryption requirements SMBs need to take payments compliantly — without hiring a security consultant or interpreting regulatory documents. Owners launch stores, accept cards, and pass basic compliance checks from day one, and you position yourself as the partner who made it easy instead of scary.

### Better search rankings as a built-in bonus
Major search engines explicitly favor encrypted sites in rankings, and modern browsers now flag non-HTTPS pages as "not secure" — tanking both SEO and conversion at once. An SSL certificate fixes both problems in a single step, bumping search visibility while removing the "not secure" warnings that scare visitors off. For SMBs investing in SEO, directory listings, or paid traffic, SSL multiplies the return on every other marketing dollar they spend.

### The right certificate for every stage of the business
Not every SMB needs enterprise-grade encryption, and not every site can get away with the basic tier. Hostopia's portfolio spans Domain Validation (DV) for informational sites, Organization Validation (OV) for commercial use, Extended Validation (EV) for high-trust ecommerce, and Wildcard certificates for domains with multiple subdomains. Customers buy exactly what fits their stage — and you upsell naturally as they grow into stores, logins, and multi-site presences.

## 3 · WHAT YOU DELIVER

**Section headline:** SSL for Every SMB Scenario — One Platform
**Intro line:** From basic blogs to high-volume ecommerce, Hostopia's SSL portfolio covers the full range of SMB security needs — with trusted certificate authority backing, branded management, and no infrastructure for you to build.

**Feature grid (6 cards):**

**Domain Validation (DV SSL)**
Entry-level encryption with domain validation and a static site seal. Ideal for blogs, informational sites, and SMBs just getting online who need the padlock without complexity.

**Organization Validation (OV SSL)**
Company-validated encryption with a dynamic site seal and higher warranty. Recommended for commercial and public-facing business sites that want verified trust signals.

**Extended Validation (EV SSL)**
The highest level of validation with full company verification and premium warranty. Built for ecommerce, login-heavy apps, and any site where trust drives revenue.

**Wildcard SSL**
Secures a domain and all subdomains (store.example.com, mail.example.com, blog.example.com) with a single certificate — reducing complexity and cost for growing businesses.

**Trusted Certificate Authorities**
All certificates issued through recognized, globally trusted CAs — with the browser compatibility and warranty backing SMB customers need, delivered entirely under your brand.

**Branded Management & Renewals**
Issuance, installation guidance, renewals, and expiration alerts all run through your partner platform — so customers never see Hostopia, and you never lose a renewal to a missed email.
