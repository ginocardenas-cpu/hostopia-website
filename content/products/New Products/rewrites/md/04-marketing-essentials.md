## 2 · ACCORDION — "Your SMB customers can…"

### Respond to every review with AI
Reviews don't wait for the weekend. Marketing Essentials uses AI to monitor Facebook, Instagram, and Google reviews in real time and draft timely, on-brand responses your customers can approve in a tap. Happy customers get acknowledged, unhappy ones get addressed before they spread, and every reply reinforces the kind of reputation that drives new business. For time-strapped owners, it turns review management from a weekly chore into something that runs quietly in the background — saving hours every month while improving trust with future buyers.

### See everything that matters in one dashboard
Most small business owners are running their business out of a dozen browser tabs. Marketing Essentials brings the numbers that actually move the needle — reviews, mentions, social performance, and ad metrics — into one clean dashboard with one login. Your customers stop switching between platforms trying to piece together what's working and start making confident, data-backed decisions fast. For your partner team, it's also one fewer support-ticket driver: less "where do I find this?" and more "what should I do next?"

### Keep listings consistent across Google, Facebook, and Instagram
When a buyer sees different hours on Google than on Facebook, trust breaks and the search ranking drops. Listings Sync keeps your customers' business details — name, address, phone, hours, website, photos — accurate and consistent across Google, Facebook, and Instagram from a single control panel. Update once, publish everywhere. Fewer missed customers, fewer embarrassing mismatches, and more of the local search visibility that drives calls, clicks, and walk-ins.

### Publish AI-powered content in minutes
Small businesses know they should post more often — they just don't have time to write it. The AI Content Creator generates on-brand posts and updates for Meta and Google in minutes, tailored to each customer's industry, tone, and goals. Schedule a month of content in an afternoon, or fire off a quick post for tonight's special. Either way, your customers stay visible to their audience without hiring an agency, and your product looks like the reason their feed finally looks alive.

### Track SEO progress without becoming an expert
Your customers don't want to learn SEO — they want to show up when someone searches. Built-in SEO Tracking grades their website against the fundamentals (titles, descriptions, speed, content, mobile) and hands back a simple, prioritized list of what to fix first. Ongoing monitoring shows whether their score is trending up or down, and how they compare to similar businesses. It's a feedback loop that turns SEO from mysterious to manageable — and turns your product into something they can't afford to cancel.
